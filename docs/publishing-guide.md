# Publishing to the KnightTek website

A short guide for posting articles, news, videos, and publications to the
**Publications** page. No coding required.

---

## 1. Sign in

1. Go to **knighttek.com/auth** (or your site address + `/auth`).
2. Enter the email and password provided by your site administrator.
3. You'll land on the **Submission Inbox**. Click **Manage Publications** in the
   top right.

> Accounts are created by an administrator. If you can't sign in, contact them.

## 2. Create a post

1. On the Publications screen, click **New post**.
2. Choose a **Content type**:
   - **Article** — a written piece with headings, images, and formatting.
   - **Press Release** — a dated announcement (same editor as an article).
   - **Video** — a YouTube or Vimeo video.
   - **Publication** — a PDF document or a link to an outside article.
3. Fill in the fields (see below).
4. Set **Status** — leave it on **Draft** until you're ready (drafts are not
   visible to the public).
5. Click **Save**.

## 3. Filling in the fields

| Field | What it's for |
| --- | --- |
| **Title** | The headline. |
| **Page address** | Auto-filled from the title — usually leave it alone. |
| **Summary** | One or two sentences shown on the listing card. |
| **Featured image** | The picture on the card and at the top of the article. Click **Upload image**. |
| **Content** (Article / Press Release) | Write here. Use the toolbar for bold, headings, lists, links, and images. |
| **Video URL** (Video) | Paste the full YouTube or Vimeo link. |
| **PDF / External link** (Publication) | Upload a PDF, paste an outside article link, or both. |

### The text editor toolbar

Bold · Italic · Heading · Subheading · Bulleted list · Numbered list · Quote ·
Link · Insert image · Undo · Redo.

To add a picture inside an article, click the **image icon** and choose a file —
it uploads and appears in your text.

## 4. Preview and publish

1. Click **Preview** at any time to see exactly how the post will look.
2. When it's ready, change **Status** to **Published**.
3. Click **Save**.

The post appears on the **Publications** page immediately — there is nothing
else to do, and no need to wait for the site to be rebuilt.

## 5. Editing or removing a post

- On the Publications screen, click any post in the list to edit it.
- To remove a post, click the **trash icon** on its row and confirm.
- To temporarily hide a published post, open it, set **Status** back to
  **Draft**, and Save.

---

**Tips**

- Save as a **Draft** often while you work.
- Keep the **Summary** short — it's the teaser on the listing page.
- Use a landscape (wide) **Featured image** for the best-looking cards.
